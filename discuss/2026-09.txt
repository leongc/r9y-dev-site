Opensource community responses to LLMs
* Giant projects (eg: Linux) and Tiny projects have different responses to LLM-based pull requests. eg: matplotlib, zig (ghosttty, block), llvm (allow but human responsibility)
* Consider Codebase and Reviewers (as resources) independently
* Continuum of how important to understand the code (LLVM vs pet project) may influence where to draw/hold a line on how to handle LLM contributions
* How can four reviewers on an open source project handle the flood of LLM contributions while balancing the longevity of the project? What are other projects seeing in policy implications and social contracts?
* Low barrier of entry to submit ideas, however low alignment with project. Code contributions vs functional contracts with community?
* Do contributions meet the standard of care for the local rules of the area of the codebase they relate to? Prioritize review of submissions by level of potential ROI according to reviewers.
